INT. HOYT HOUSE - KENDRA'S ROOM - EVENING

SUPER: EARLIER. HALE RIVER, ALABAMA. 2014.

A small room. The window unit labors. Tops on the bed, three
deep. BRIANNA, 16, round-faced and sure of herself, her
hair done and her shoes already on, sits cross-legged on
the bed with Kendra's phone, scrolling. KENDRA HOYT, 16,
her father's long frame already, headphones around her
neck, is half inside the closet.

                    KENDRA
          This one?

She holds up a top. Brianna does not look up.

                    BRIANNA
          Yeah.

                    KENDRA
          You didn't look.

Brianna looks.

                    BRIANNA
          Yeah. That one.

                    KENDRA
          Or this.

                    BRIANNA
          That's a church top.

                    KENDRA
          It is not a church top. I wore it
          to the thing.

                    BRIANNA
          You wore it to church and then to
          the thing.

Kendra throws it on the bed with the others and goes back
in. Brianna goes back to the phone.

                    BRIANNA
          Marcus is going.

From inside the closet:

                    KENDRA
          Who said.

                    BRIANNA
          Tasha said Keisha said. Keisha's in
          Darnell's fourth period, and
          Darnell sits by Marcus, and Darnell
          told Keisha he was going because
          Marcus asked him was he going. So
          he's going.

Kendra comes out with a third top and holds it against
herself in the mirror on the closet door. Brianna is behind
her in the mirror, still on the phone.

                    KENDRA
          Marcus asked Darnell.

                    BRIANNA
          No. Marcus asked Jalen was Darnell
          going, and Jalen asked Darnell, and
          Darnell said he'd go if Marcus was
          going, and Jalen told Marcus that,
          and Marcus said then he'd go. So
          they're both going. Because of each
          other.

Kendra turns the top one way and the other in the mirror.

                    KENDRA
          He sits in front of me third period
          and every time she passes papers
          back he turns all the way around.
          Every time. He's got nothing to
          say, he just turns around.

Brianna puts the phone down.

                    BRIANNA
          He's looking at you.

                    KENDRA
          He's looking at Tasha.

                    BRIANNA
          Tasha sits by the door.

Kendra looks at herself with the top a while longer.

                    KENDRA
          This one.

                    BRIANNA
          That one. Did your daddy say he's
          driving?

Kendra pulls the top she has on off over her head and the
new one on. The next lines come through fabric.

                    KENDRA
          He's driving.

                    BRIANNA
          My mama's going to call him.

                    KENDRA
          She called Miss Ondine.

                    BRIANNA
          She called Miss Ondine and Miss
          Ondine talked to her forty-five
          minutes about her hip.

Kendra's head comes out of the new top.

                    KENDRA
          Which hip.

                    BRIANNA
          I don't know which hip, Kendra.

The front door, off. Boots on the floor. Brianna is off the
bed before Kendra is, and hands her the phone on the way
past.

                    HOYT (O.S.)
          Kendra.

                    KENDRA
          In here.

INT. HOYT HOUSE - KITCHEN - CONTINUOUS

AMOS HOYT, 40s, Black, lean, in a work shirt with the
sleeves rolled, sets a toolbox down inside the door and goes to the sink. He runs
the water and starts on his hands with the bar soap. The
girls come in behind him. Brianna sits at the table without
being asked. Kendra stops in the doorway in the new top.

                    BRIANNA
          Hi, Mr. Hoyt.

                    HOYT
          Brianna. Your mama find Ondine?

                    BRIANNA
          Yes sir. They talked about her hip.

                    HOYT
          Which hip.

                    BRIANNA
          I don't --

                    KENDRA
          Nobody knows which hip, Daddy.

He shuts the water off and turns with the towel, and sees
the two of them. The top.

                    HOYT
          What are y'all into.

                    KENDRA
          Nothing.

                    HOYT
          That's a lot of nothing.

                    KENDRA
          We're going to the movies. You
          said.

                    HOYT
          I said I'd drive. What movie.

Kendra comes the rest of the way in and leans on the counter
beside him.

                    KENDRA
          The scary one. Everybody's going.

                    HOYT
          Who's everybody.

                    KENDRA
          Everybody.

                    BRIANNA
          Tasha, and Darnell, and Marcus, and
          --

                    KENDRA
          Everybody.

                    HOYT
          Marcus.

He opens the refrigerator and stands in it.

                    HOYT
          That's the Shelby boy. I put a belt
          on his mama's dryer. He stood in
          that laundry room the whole time I
          was down on the floor and never
          once handed me a thing. I'd say
          hand me that, and he'd look at it.

                    BRIANNA
          He's shy.

                    HOYT
          He's something.

                    KENDRA
          Daddy.

                    HOYT
          I'm saying what I saw.

He takes out the tea and pours a glass. Drinks half of it
standing there.

                    HOYT
          What time's it start.

                    KENDRA
          Seven-thirty.

He pulls out the chair across from Brianna and sits down
with the glass, the first time he has sat since he came in.

                    HOYT
          I might go see that.

                    KENDRA
          No.

                    BRIANNA
          He could sit in the back.

                    KENDRA
          Brianna.

                    HOYT
          I'll sit in the back. I'll sit with
          Marcus. Hand him some popcorn, see
          does he take it.

Brianna laughs. Kendra doesn't, and then does. She is the
only one standing.

                    KENDRA
          You're driving. You're not going
          in.

                    HOYT
          We'll see what I do.

He gets up with the tea and heads for the hall. In the
doorway he turns back and looks at the top again.

                    HOYT
          You going in that?

                    KENDRA
          What's wrong with it.

                    HOYT
          Nothing's wrong with it.

He looks at it a second longer than that.

                    HOYT
          Seven-thirty.

                    KENDRA
          Seven-thirty.

                    HOYT
          Then somebody better start supper.
          I ain't driving nobody hungry.

Kendra is already at the refrigerator before he has finished
saying it. He goes down the hall. Brianna stays where she is
at the table, like she lives there.
