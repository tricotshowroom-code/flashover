1. WHAT THE AUDIENCE GETS NOW, AND WHAT IS MISSING
June appears four times before the fire. She speaks in two.
- 2.1 (M13; Draft 03 working). Rehearsal at half-voice; the buzz; "You fix amps now?"; the flat exchange; Hoyt stays to listen; Deena's keys. Gives: her exactness, her impatience with herself, a crossed understanding with Hoyt repaired at once, and his attention to her. Does not give: her full voice (the draft's action claims "more in it than the room needs," which is a claim, not a thing heard), or any sign she is going anywhere.
- 2.5 (M19; Draft 02 approved). June at the hose line in a coat over her nightclothes, a neighbor's hand on her shoulder, firelight on her face. No speech. Her presence on that street is unexplained in the Master and stays so.
- 2.9 (M23; REWRITE pending). Voice only. She wants the room Thursday for two new songs; she learns Hoyt is let go; "Nine years, Amos."; "Friday's my last show." / "Come anyway." Gives the single best piece of June on the page: her own news stopping dead when his trouble arrives (her card's processing silence, already in the Master as "Nothing on the line."). Does not give her face.
- 2.12 (M27; PORT pending). Apron to bandstand; "Tray or that guitar."; the dedication to Ray and Ondine; "Can't. Not won't."; the slow number begins; cut. Gives: her ease with a room, how she includes people by name, the band. Does not give sustained singing; the Master cuts to the dock as the number starts, and the blueprint cuts to the lake.
After the fire: the photograph laughing at something outside the frame, the guitarist with no cable, Ondine humming the slow number (4.4); Hoyt's "June asked me to come." (M48); Ondine wanting Hoyt to talk about June and his refusal (5.4); and at 7.5, reserved to the end: ten o'clock, June bringing Deena water, their words under the music, both laughing; quarter to eleven, June alone on the bandstand humming something slow, her boot coming down, the band hitting; midnight-fifteen, she stops singing mid-line.
What is missing, in order of weight: (1) her voice, heard whole, before the fire, which the Guide requires of an existing pre-fire Magnolia scene; (2) one encounter where she is not working, so that we know how she talks when nothing is being fixed or served; (3) the arrangement as a lived fact from her own mouth, in logistics, which the Guide says is how she tells it, and which the pages never give (the Master's one line about her future is "four more months of tips"); (4) June and Deena as a pair, which the Guide builds in detail and the pages give one exchange; (5) the representative (NEW DIRECTION). Not missing: Hoyt. The three Hoyt encounters are reciprocal already and should not be added to.

3. THE REPRESENTATIVE (NEW DIRECTION; everything below is the minimal setup, SUGGESTED)
Established constraints kept: the producer who heard her recording, booked the two songs and is paying the flight (Guide, LOCKED); no record deal or success established; nineteen dead, unchanged.
Who he is: OPEN by the brief. Recommendation: another person, not the producer. The producer has already committed money on the recordings; a man who flies in to watch her hold a room is the label's, evaluating what a recording cannot show. That is what the brief says his purpose is. His role and name stay OPEN; he needs no name on screen.
Setup before Friday: one sentence, once. Either in 2.7A (June to Deena, as a logistic among logistics: "there's a man from the label coming Friday to see me do it in a room"; Deena: "What room?"; June: "This one.") or in 2.12 only, through Deena ("He here?" / "Table by the post."). Recommendation: 2.7A, so that Friday carries it without saying it. Not both.
His presence Friday: Pete on the door calls every name coming in; the one name he does not call is this man's, because he does not know it. The man sits alone at a table by the post with a drink he does not drink much of, and watches the bandstand, not the room. June sees him once as she unclips her apron, and then does exactly what she was going to do. No introduction, no handshake, no line between them. The dedication, the slow number and the room are hers; he is a man at a table. After the number: one look from him, which could mean anything, and the scene leaves for the lake.
His death: the count stays nineteen. He is one of the fourteen the Master never names (named dead: June, Pete, Deena; the five at the door unassigned by name). How it becomes clear, recommended at 3.4, the stretchers: among the county's pickups in the lot, a sedan with an airport rental tag in the windshield that nobody claims as the sheets go out, and the deputy with the clipboard writing something and asking a survivor who it belongs to, and getting a shrug. Visual, under a page, no line about the label. Alternative: Ondine's statement at the apparatus bay could name "the man from the record company at the table by the post" among the dead; that is a line and the Writer prefers the car. Whether a morning meeting or signing was scheduled: OPEN by the brief; recommendation, leave it unscheduled. The Ty line already carries a tomorrow that does not come, and a second one would be a device.
